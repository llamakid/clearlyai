AI can hand you a full website in minutes. That's the good news. The bad news is that "it looks finished" and "it actually works" are not the same thing — and the gap between them is exactly where AI tools tend to cut corners.

Before you share the link with a single customer, run through this checklist. It takes about five minutes.

## 1. Open it on your phone

Most AI-generated sites look great on the wide desktop screen they were designed on. Then you open it on a phone and the menu is broken, text overlaps, or a button is unreachable.

**Do this:** Pull up your site on your actual phone, not just a "mobile preview" in a browser. Try every page, every button, every form.

## 2. Click every link

AI tools sometimes generate placeholder links — "#" or a page that doesn't exist yet — especially for things like "Contact," "Privacy Policy," or footer links.

**Do this:** Click literally every link on every page. If something is a placeholder, either build that page or remove the link. A dead link on a business site reads as "this company doesn't sweat the details."

## 3. Check how fast it loads

AI-generated sites often come loaded with big, uncompressed images or unnecessary code the tool added "just in case." A slow site loses visitors before they see anything.

**Do this:** Use a free tool like [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev) — paste in your URL and see the score. If it flags huge images, that's usually the fix: compress them or ask your AI tool to do it.

## 4. Look for accessibility basics

Does every image have alt text? Can you navigate the site using only your keyboard (tab key)? Is the text contrast readable — light gray text on a white background looks trendy but is hard for a lot of people to read.

**Do this:** Try tabbing through your homepage without touching the mouse. If you get stuck or lost, a visitor using a screen reader will too.

## 5. Read every word of the copy

AI writes confidently — even when it's wrong. It might invent a phone number format, misstate your hours, describe a service you don't actually offer, or just sound generic (more on that below).

**Do this:** Read the entire site out loud, line by line, as if you're a customer seeing it for the first time. Anything that sounds "off" or made-up, fix it or cut it.

## The pattern here

None of these are hard to catch — they just require someone to actually look. AI is fast at generating; it's not naturally careful about checking its own work. That job is still yours.

Five minutes of checking now saves you an embarrassing "our website says we're open 24/7 and we're not" conversation later.
